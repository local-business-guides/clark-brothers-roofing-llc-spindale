Questions to cover before work begins

Roofing work can mean different things depending on the condition and needs of a home. Ask the contractor to describe the issue, the work they recommend, and what is not included. If there are choices to make, ask what each option addresses so you can decide with a clear picture of the job.

A short checklist can keep the conversation on track:

  • Describe what you have seen and when you first noticed it.
  • Ask what areas of the roof the work will cover.
  • Confirm what preparation or access is needed at your home.
  • Find out how the work area and cleanup will be handled.
  • Make sure you understand the agreed scope before work starts.

Avoid assuming that a small visible problem tells the whole story. Also, do not accept a description you cannot follow. Ask for clarification before making a decision.
